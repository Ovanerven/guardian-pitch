The script is the spine

The slide exists to support the script, so the slide follows the script’s order: hook, problem, product, privacy, proof, business, ask.

Two issues with the current script:

  • Too long. 253 words is about 100 s at a calm stage pace. The target is about 210 words.
  • It skips what makes us strongest: the customer conversations, the privacy pivot, and the team.

Script v1 (current, 253 words)

If a stranger talked to your kid at the park, you’d ask who they are. So why don’t we ask the same question when a stranger talks to them online? This is why we are building Guardian Agent.

Online gaming is more dangerous than ever. Kids are playing games at increasingly younger ages, while the threats of online gaming are only growing. Online grooming, where adults try to meet kids they met online, has never been more common.

No product that exists today meets what parents need. Today’s tools are poorly suited to catching online threats in real time.

Meet Guardian Agent, a desktop application we have developed over the last half a year. It sits on your child’s PC, listens to voice and text chat, and understands the context of each game and conversation. It detects grooming in real time, alerting parents the moment something’s wrong.

What is behind this? We have fine-tuned and distilled an AI model that is highly accurate while being incredibly lightweight. This lets your child play games at full performance, all while the data never leaves their device.

We have given our software to parents from three pilot schools, and are using their feedback to improve Guardian Agent continuously.

The launch is planned for next month, and this is our pricing.

So will you help us achieve our dream of a world where children can play and connect online without risk? Sign up for the beta using this QR code now. Thank you.

Script v2 (suggested, about 210 words, same story plus pivot and team)

If a stranger talked to your kid at the park, you’d ask who they are. So why don’t we ask the same question when a stranger talks to them online?

Kids are gaming younger than ever, and online grooming has grown 89% in six years. No product today catches it in real time.

So we built Guardian Agent: a desktop app on your child’s PC that understands voice and text chat in every game, and alerts parents the moment something’s wrong.

Then we talked to parents. In every conversation, one question came up first: where does my child’s data go? So we made privacy the architecture. We fine-tuned and distilled our own AI model that runs entirely on the child’s PC. Accurate, lightweight, no game lag, and nothing leaves the device.

Today parents at three international schools use it, and their feedback shapes every release. They also told us what they would pay, so plans start at €6.99 a month.

We’re Marom and Oliver. We both graduated with an MSc in AI, Marom is a founding engineer at Biscuit, I’m an AI engineer at Prosus, and we trained this model ourselves. We launch next month.

Help us build a world where children can play online without risk. Scan the code and join the beta.

Style rules (from the team):

  • Concise. Distil to the main points, with no over-explaining in words.
  • No “·” bullets between words, no em dashes, no long sentences.
  • Visuals over text. Readable from the back of the room: nothing under ~28 px at 1920 wide.
  • Not necessarily the dark theme. Try light too.

Q&A prep (4 minutes)

Likely question Short answer
Who is your primary customer? Parents of 8 to 14 year olds who game on PC. Schools buying bulk licences next.
How do you know the problem exists? NSPCC: grooming crimes up 89% in 6 years. Every parent we spoke to recognised it, and privacy came up in every conversation.
Why not existing tools? They are rule-based keyword filters, per game, and reactive (weeks later or never). Competitors upload the child’s voice to the cloud.
How do you make money? Monthly subscription. Inference runs on the child’s PC, so the cost to serve is cents per user.
Results so far? Working app, 3 pilot schools, a parent feedback group, pricing validated, launch next month.
Biggest challenge? Distribution: reaching parents at scale. Schools are our wedge.
Next step? Public launch next month, then the school channel and Mac support.
Why you? Both MSc AI graduates working as engineers (Marom: Founding Engineer at Biscuit, Oliver: AI Engineer at Prosus). We built and trained the model ourselves, and we grew up gaming.
